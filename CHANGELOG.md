# Changelog

## v1.1

- Additional screen bezel option to accomodate for larger 1280x800 display, redesigned back bezel to accomodate
- SAO ports on front bezel
- Extended screen bezel bottom corners and removed need for keyboard veeners at top
- Minor changes to keyboard tray to mount Pimoroni trackball breakout with RGBW + click
- Extended bottom footprint (keyboard tray and screen bezel) approx 4mm on bottom to accomodate rubber isolator standoffs for a quieter typing experience
- Added cut outs for front and side USB ports with room for ribbon cable, can connect port directly to keyboard for use as external keyboard, or as extensions to the built-in RPI ports 
- Case for protection while in transit
